WHAT WE SET UP & OPERATE

A working AI practice, not another subscription.

For owners who want AI working for them, not another subscription — trades, home services, solo professionals, and small firms. Six services that stand alone or work together, every one tracked in your own private portal.

01

Custom AI, built around you

A private assistant set up on your own machine or in your own accounts, working from your prices, your services, your documents, and the answers you already give — plus custom tools shaped to how you actually work.

02

Intake and follow-up that run themselves

Calls, forms and messages captured and sorted the moment they arrive; quotes chased, appointments confirmed, past customers reminded when it’s time again — so a job is never lost because you were out working.

03

Research that files itself

Suppliers, competitors, pricing, and whatever else you keep meaning to look into — gathered, sourced, and summarized on a schedule, so you are not doing it at ten at night.

Your Google Business Profile set up and verified properly, a page of your own with an estimate form, and every inquiry tracked from the first click to the customer — so you know what actually brought them in.

Everything we set up records what it did and what came of it — an inquiry and whether it became a customer, an answer and whether it was right, a run and whether a person had to step in. 06

Coaching
