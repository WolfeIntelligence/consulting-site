A private assistant on your own machine, or in your own accounts. It works from your prices, your services, your documents, and the answers you already give. Plus custom tools built for the way you work, not the way software wishes you did.

One-on-one, until the thing stops feeling like a thing. I won’t use a word you’d have to look up. There’s no homework. And you’re not supposed to need me forever. That’s the point of doing it this way.
