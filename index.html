IP-based equity research.

The same read I do on patents, turned on public companies. Which companies are actually building what in the AI buildout, according to their claims rather than their press releases. Who is filing where, how fast, and whether the claims cover the thing the market says they cover.

How it is built

Classification first, keyword search second. The CPC is an ontology, and a landscape built on it shows position; a phrase search only shows vocabulary. Every count traces back to the query that produced it, and every reading of a claim quotes the claim.

I’m Zachary Wolfe, Zach to most people. Physics degree, electrical engineering background. My day job is reading about twenty patents a day and deciding where each one belongs.
