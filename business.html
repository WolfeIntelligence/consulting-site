WHAT I SET UP

Six pieces. Take one or take several.

Each one works on its own. Together they cover how a customer finds you, what happens when they call, and what you learn from it.

01

Custom AI, built around you

A private assistant on your own computer or in your own accounts, working from your prices, your services, your documents and the answers you already give customers. I also build tools for the parts of the job regular software doesn’t handle. Usually that is pricing.

02

Intake and follow-up that run themselves

A call comes in while you’re on a job. It gets caught, sorted and answered. Same for forms and texts. Quotes get followed up, appointments get confirmed, and last year’s customers hear from you when the work comes around again.

03

Research that files itself

Suppliers, competitors, pricing, whatever you keep meaning to look into. Gathered on a schedule and written up, with a source on every line.

04

Get found on Google

Your Google Business Profile set up and verified, and a page of your own with an estimate form on it. Every inquiry tracked from the first click to the paying customer, so you know which search, ad or listing brought them in.

05

A system that learns from your results

Did the inquiry become a customer? Was the answer right? Did someone have to step in? All of it goes back into the setup, so month two is built on what happened in month one.

06

Coaching

One-on-one on video, on your real jobs, until you can run it without me. No jargon and no homework.
